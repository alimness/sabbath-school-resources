**Paper People From Different Countries**

**What you need:**

Paper people cutouts, craft glue, craft sticks, fabric/colored paper, tape, scissors, and pictures of children from different countries for inspiration (1).

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out people shapes in various skin tones.
- Print pictures of children from different countries or have a picture book to show.
- Cut up small pieces of fabric ready to use.

**Create it together:**

- Stick on fabrics and/or colored paper to “dress” the paper people (2).
- Attach a craft stick to the back of the people with tape (3).
