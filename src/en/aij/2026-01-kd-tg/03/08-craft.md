**Sabbath Vase Picture**

**What you need:**

**Option 1:** white cardstock, green paper strips, colored fabrics, colored tissue paper, craft glue, and scissors (1).\
**Option 2:** small glass jars, acrylic paints, paintbrush, water, and smocks (to cover clothing) (Option 2 [1]).

**Preparation Instructions:**

- Cut out a vase shape in the fabric.
- Cut green strips of paper.

**Create it together:**

- Encourage children to create a special vase of flowers just for Sabbath.
- Glue vertical lines and stick sections of the green paper strips to make wavy stems, as shown (2).
- Glue fabric vase to page, so it overlaps the bottom of each stem (3).
- Create colorful flowers by gluing scrunched up pieces of colored tissue paper (4).
- Children can share their special vase picture with someone next week to open Sabbath.
- Alternatively, if you have access to small glass jars, children can paint the jars with acrylic paints to use as a Sabbath vase (Option 2 [2]).
