**What you need:**

Colored paper, pencil, scissors, tape, green and yellow pipe cleaners/chenille sticks.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](11a.png)

```=Craft Template

![](craft-template.png)

```

**Instructions:**

1\. Trace around your hand on a piece of colored paper.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](11b.png)

2\. Cut out the handprint and bend into a cone shape. Tape the lower part of the thumb and little finger together, leaving a small opening at the bottom of the cone. Roll the fingers around a pencil to curl the fingers like petals.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](11c.png)

3\. Cut a yellow pipe cleaner/chenille stick in half and bend it together with a green one.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](11d.png)

4\. Wind the rest of the pipe cleaners/chenille sticks around until there are no sharp ends.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](11e.png)

5\. Slide the pipe cleaner “stem” through the center of the handprint lily.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](11f.png)
