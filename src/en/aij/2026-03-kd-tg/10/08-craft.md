**Create it together:**

1\. Color in the basket and cut out.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](10b.png)

2\. Fold the basket horizontally along the middle, then tape or glue just the sides together to make a pocket.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](10c.png)

3\. Cut out the “loaves and fish” cards and slide them into the “basket.” Retell the story to someone and show them how the loaves and fish multiplied, as you pull out one card at a time.
