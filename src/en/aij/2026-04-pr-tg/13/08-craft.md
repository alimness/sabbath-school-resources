**What you need:**

Week 13 craft template, colored pencils/markers, scissors, glue, black paper, tape.

**Preparation Instructions:**

Cut pieces of black paper 4.5 x 7.2 inches (11.5 x 18.5 centimeters).

**Instructions:**

1\. Color in the tomb picture and cut around the outside. An adult can help cut out the doorway if needed.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13b.png)

2\. Fold along the dotted line at the bottom and tape just the ends.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13c.png)

3\. Flip over and glue a black piece of paper to the top half of the page.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13d.png)

4\. Fold along the top and middle lines. Tape the ends together to make an A-frame.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13e.png)

5\. Cut out and color the stone and Lazarus. Fold along the lines and glue to flap to make Lazarus stand up.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13f.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13g.png)

6\. Put Lazarus inside the doorway of the tomb and slide the stone in front.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13h.png)

7\. Reenact the story as you roll away the stone and make Lazarus come out!

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](13i.png)
