**What you need:**

Week 4 craft template on cardstock, scissors, glue, craft knife (adults only), craft stick, tape, sharp pencil, two craft split pins.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](4a.png)

```=Craft Template

![](craft-template-a.png)

![](craft-template-b.png)

```

**Preparation Instructions:**

Use a craft knife to make a slit along the dotted line on the water.

**Instructions:**

1\. Cut out the pieces. Glue Jesus and the boat onto the sea background.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](4b.png)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](4c.png)

2\. Tape a craft stick to the back of Peter’s picture.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](4d.png)

3\. Use a sharp pencil to carefully poke a hole through the white dots and insert two craft split pins —one through Jesus’ arm, and the other through Jesus’ and Peter’s hand.

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](4e.png)

(Note: you may like to fold the back of the craft split pin so it can’t be seen behind the hand.)

{"style":{"image":{"aspectRatio":1.778}}}
![](4f.png)

4\. Slide Peter through the slit in the water and move him up with the craft stick to show Jesus lifting him out of the water!
