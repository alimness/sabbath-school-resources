Use this ^[prepare]({"style":{"text":{"color": "#2A3D78", "typeface": "Gibson-SemiBold"}}}) log to plan and record acts of kindness you do for your neighbors, coworkers, family, and other people in your life. Write down both the name of the individuals you are interacting with and the activity itself. Consider writing down needs that exist in your community and how you intend to get involved. Include how you plan to befriend your neighbors or coworkers. Schedule to have someone over for dinner or to go on a walk together. Take a personal interest in those around you.
